#### **Data Management Week meets Programming Café:** Learn how to manage your data using research software 
In this session, Data Stewards and Research Software trainers will collaborate to show you how good data management and practical coding go hand in hand. You will learn how to plan, structure, and validate your research data, how to share it responsibly, and how to collaborate more effectively with the help of Python. The session will give you concrete tools to make your data more organized, reusable, and FAIR‑friendly. 
 
The second part of the session is hands-on. Are you stuck on a problem? Not sure how to apply what you’ve learned? Need some fresh ideas? Bring your code, your bugs, or your ideas and get practical help from EUR’s Research Software trainers. 

Want to download the jupyter notebook directly? Find it **[here](extras/DataManagementwithSoftware.ipynb)**.
